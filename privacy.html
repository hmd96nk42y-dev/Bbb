Privacy policy

BUBL · bubl concierge · Last updated 24 November 2026

BUBL helps guests book tables and plan nights at partner venues, through our web app and our WhatsApp assistant, bubl. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We are currently running a pilot.

What we collect

  • Your name, mobile number and email address.
  • Your bookings: the venue, night, table, party size and who you invite.
  • The messages you send to bubl on WhatsApp, and bubl's replies.
  • Payment status from our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.

Why we use it

  • To make, change and confirm your bookings and to answer your messages.
  • To share your booking details with the venue you booked, so they can welcome you.
  • To keep the service safe and to improve it.

Who we share it with

Only the venue you book with, and the service providers that run BUBL for us (hosting, messaging through WhatsApp, and payments). We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

For as long as you use BUBL and as the law requires, then we delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your data at any time. Write to samk@thebublapp.com. To stop messages from bubl, block or delete the chat in WhatsApp.
